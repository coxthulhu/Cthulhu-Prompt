title: "Example: Q&A with Implementation & Testing"
---
In this session, we are designing and implementing a feature. Here are the steps you should follow:
1. Read over the relevant code, including the relevant integration tests.
2. Before you make any changes, tell me your initial design and respond to me with a short numbered list of the questions you have.
    - For each question, include a line for your reasoning on anything not specified in my prompt and a line with your suggested answer.
    - If your question pertains to a specific requirement, give its number.
    - Do not proceed until I have answered all of your questions and you have answered all of my questions.
3. After we have answered each other's questions, make the required code changes.
    - Keep things simple, write as little code as possible, and avoid defensive programming.
    - Feel free to change existing code, and update variable and function names to match the updated functionality.
4. After your code changes are complete, run the unit and integration tests.
5. Concisely summarize the results of your development. Include a brief mention of any new test cases and your reasoning behind adding them. Let me know if any integration test runs failed during development, and why.
